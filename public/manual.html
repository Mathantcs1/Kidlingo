2. Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your performance command centre. It loads pre-computed statistics from all your trades and refreshes automatically when you add or edit a trade.

Stat Cards

CardWhat It Shows
Total P&LSum of profit/loss across all closed trades
Win RatePercentage of trades that ended in profit
Profit FactorGross profit ÷ gross loss (above 1.5 is strong)
Avg R:RAverage reward-to-risk ratio per trade
Max DrawdownLargest peak-to-trough drop in equity (%)
Sharpe RatioRisk-adjusted return (above 1 is acceptable)

Charts

  • Equity Curve — running cumulative P&L over time with gradient fill
  • Daily P&L Bar Chart — green bars for profit days, red for loss days
  • Calendar Heatmap — monthly grid coloured by daily P&L magnitude; navigate months with arrows
  • Instrument Breakdown — donut chart of trade count by instrument
  • Best & Worst Trades — top 3 wins and worst 3 losses at a glance

3. Managing Trades

Viewing Your Trades

Click Trades in the left sidebar. The table shows all trades sorted by entry date (newest first) with colour-coded P&L (green = profit, red = loss).

Filtering

Use the filter bar at the top to narrow by:

  • Date range (from / to)
  • Instrument (e.g. AAPL, BTC/USD)
  • Direction (Long / Short)
  • Status (Open / Closed)
  • Strategy tag

Sorting

Click any column header to sort ascending; click again for descending.

Creating a Trade

1
Click New Trade (top-right on the Trades page) or navigate to /trades/new.
2
Fill in the required fields: Instrument, Direction, Entry Price, Quantity, Entry Date.
3
Optionally add Exit Price & Date, Stop Loss, Take Profit, Commission, Strategy Tag, Session Type, Psychology notes, and a screenshot.
4
Watch the Live P&L Preview panel update as you type — it shows estimated P&L and R:R ratio in real time.
5
Click Save Trade. P&L and R-Multiple are calculated server-side and stored.
Note: Leaving Exit Price empty saves the trade as OPEN. Fill it in later by editing the trade to mark it closed.

Editing a Trade

From the Trades table, click the ⋮ menu on any row → Edit. All fields are editable. P&L is recalculated on save.

Deleting a Trade

Click ⋮ → Delete. Deletion is permanent.

Trade Detail View

Click any trade row to open its detail page. You will see:

  • Entry/Exit Timeline Chart — visualises entry, stop loss, take profit, and exit price levels
  • AI Analysis panel — click Analyse Trade to get AI coaching feedback
  • Full trade metadata including notes and psychology journal

5. Daily Trade Plans

Trade plans are pre-market setups you create before executing a trade. They enforce discipline by forcing you to define criteria upfront.

Creating a Plan

1
Click Plans in the sidebar → New Plan.
2
Set the Trading Date, Instrument, Direction, Entry Price, Stop Loss, Take Profit.
3
Optionally write a Rationale explaining why you are taking this trade.
4
Toggle Enable Price Alerts ON to automatically create alerts for your entry, SL, and TP levels.
5
Click Save Plan. Three price alerts are created automatically.

Plan Statuses

StatusMeaning
PENDINGPlan created, waiting for price to reach entry
TRIGGEREDEntry price was hit — time to execute the trade
EXPIREDPlan date passed without triggering
CANCELLEDManually cancelled before triggering

Converting a Plan to a Trade

Open a plan's detail page and click Convert to Trade. The trade form opens pre-filled with the plan's instrument, direction, prices, and dates. Review, add exit details, and save.

6. Price Alerts

Price alerts notify you when an instrument reaches a target price. They are checked every 30 seconds while the app is open in your browser.

How Alerts Work

  1. Alerts are created automatically when you save a Trade Plan with alerts enabled.
  2. You can also create manual alerts from Alerts in the sidebar.
  3. Every 30 seconds the app checks current prices via Yahoo Finance / Alpha Vantage.
  4. When a price crosses a threshold a toast notification appears on screen.
  5. The alert status changes to TRIGGERED in the alerts table.

Alert Types

TypeTriggers When
ENTRY_HITPrice reaches your planned entry level
STOP_HITPrice reaches your stop loss level
EXIT_HITPrice reaches your take profit level
PRICE_ABOVEPrice rises above a custom target
PRICE_BELOWPrice falls below a custom target

Managing Alerts

  • Check Now — manually force a price check immediately
  • Dismiss — mark a triggered alert as acknowledged
  • Dismiss All — clear all triggered alerts at once
Note: Price data depends on external APIs. Free-tier Alpha Vantage allows 25 requests/day. For live trading use, add your own Alpha Vantage API key in Vercel environment variables (ALPHA_VANTAGE_API_KEY).

7. AI Analysis & Chat

The app integrates with both Claude (Anthropic) and GPT-4o (OpenAI). You choose your preferred provider in Settings.

Requires API key: AI features need ANTHROPIC_API_KEY or OPENAI_API_KEY set in the Vercel environment variables. Contact your admin if AI is not working.

Per-Trade Analysis

1
Open any trade detail page.
2
Scroll to the AI Analysis panel and click Analyse Trade.
3
The AI returns feedback covering pattern recognition, risk assessment, psychology observations, and 3 specific improvement suggestions.
4
Results are cached — re-opening the same trade shows the stored analysis instantly without using another API call.

Portfolio Summary

On the AI page (sidebar), click Weekly Summary or Monthly Summary to generate an AI overview of your recent performance covering strengths, weaknesses, and 3 action items.

AI Chat

The chat interface on the AI page lets you ask questions about your trading performance. Responses stream in real time. Example questions:

  • "What is my best-performing strategy?"
  • "Why do I lose more on Fridays?"
  • "How can I improve my risk management?"

Switching AI Provider

Go to Settings → Profile → AI Provider and select either Claude or GPT-4o. Save changes. All subsequent AI calls use the new provider.

8. Settings & Customisation

Access via Settings in the sidebar or your avatar menu.

Profile Tab

  • Full Name — update your display name
  • Email — read-only (cannot be changed)
  • AI Provider — choose Claude or GPT-4o

Custom Fields Tab

Every dropdown in the trade form uses your personal list of values. You can customise five categories:

CategoryUsed In
Strategy TagsTrade form — Strategy field
Instruments / WatchlistTrade form — Instrument field
Psychology TagsTrade form — Psychology field
Trade SetupsTrade form — Setup field
Session TypesTrade form — Session field

Adding a Value

Type the name in the input box at the bottom of each category section and press Enter or click the + button.

Setting a Default

Click the ☆ (star) icon on any value to make it the pre-selected default in new trade forms.

Deleting a Value

Click the 🗑 (trash) icon. Existing trades that used this value are not affected.

9. Admin Panel

Only users with the ADMIN role can access /admin.

User Management

Navigate to Admin → Users to see all registered users. You can:

  • Change a user's role (ADMIN / TRADER / VIEWER) using the dropdown in the table
  • View each user's email, registration date, and current plan
Viewer role: Users with the VIEWER role can see data but cannot create, edit, or delete trades.

Subscription Management

Navigate to Admin → Subscriptions to manage plans for all users:

  • Change a user's plan (FREE / BASIC / PRO) using the dropdown
  • Changes take effect immediately on the user's next request

Groups

Navigate to Admin → Groups to view trading groups. Groups allow multiple traders to share a common trade log.

11. Keyboard Shortcuts & Tips

Navigation Tips

  • Click the ≡ button in the sidebar header to collapse it and gain more screen space.
  • On mobile, the sidebar slides in as a drawer — tap the hamburger icon in the header.
  • The theme toggle (sun/moon icon in the header) switches between dark and light mode.

Trade Entry Tips

  • Use the Live P&L Preview panel on the right side of the trade form to validate your risk before saving.
  • If you pre-filled a trade from a Trade Plan, all plan fields are carried over automatically.
  • You can upload a chart screenshot to the trade for future reference.

Dashboard Tips

  • Click the ← → arrows on the Calendar Heatmap to navigate months.
  • Hover over any chart bar or point for exact values.

AI Tips

  • Analysis results are cached — closing and reopening a trade detail page does not use another API call.
  • Switch providers in Settings if one API key is not working.
  • The AI chat has context about your account — ask it questions like "How many trades did I win this month?"
