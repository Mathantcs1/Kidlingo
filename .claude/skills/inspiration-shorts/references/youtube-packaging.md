# YouTube packaging (titles, descriptions, tags, upload settings)

Evidence: in the Pride Parables export the two best Shorts had plain curiosity titles ("When the Competitor You Mocked Buys Your Company", "The King Who Said One Word Too Many"); the weakest had hashtags inside the title or a generic stake ("... It Cost Him Everything"). Confounded by publish date and tiny counts, but it matches how the feed works: the title and the first frame are all a swiping viewer sees.

Titles (under 60 characters)
- State the conflict or the irony with one specific detail ("He Left for Work at Nine. He Had No Work."). Question form is fine when the number is shaky ("McDonald's: Burger Company or Landlord?").
- No hashtags in the title; no generic stakes ("It Cost Him Everything", "You Won't Believe"); do not promise what the video does not show.
- Write two alternates; use the one that names a person, a number or a contradiction.
Description
- Line 1 is the hook sentence again; line 2 the question; then Fiction / sources / "commentary, not affiliated" / AI-narration note; exactly three hashtags last (only the first three show above the title).
- Brandwake: as-of date, source list and computed figures explained. Pride Parables: the owner's own "Why I made this:" sentence.
Tags: 8-12 relevant tags, under 500 characters; they matter little for Shorts.
Pinned comment: the question, the sources (Brandwake), the tagline (Pride Parables). Post it yourself right after publishing and reply to early comments.
Studio settings: altered or synthetic content = Yes (AI narration); not made for kids; schedule, one per channel per day; never upload the same story twice.
