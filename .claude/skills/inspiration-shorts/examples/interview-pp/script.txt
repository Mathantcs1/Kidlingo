He lied about one skill. Then they handed him the keyboard.
Ace's résumé listed five skills. Four were true. The fifth said: advanced Excel.
Pride had insisted. "Nobody checks. Everyone writes advanced."
The interview went well. Ace was funny. Ace was calm. Pride was glowing.
Then the manager, Ms. Verma, turned her laptop around. "Great. Build me a pivot table. Right now."
Ace looked at the screen. The screen looked back.
"Easy," Pride whispered. "Click things confidently."
He clicked. A chart appeared. Nobody knew why. Five minutes. His shirt was wet.
"Say the software crashed," said Pride.
Ace's hand hovered over the keyboard. Then he took it away.
"I can't do this," he said. "I wrote advanced. I'm a beginner. I can learn it by Friday. But I shouldn't have written that."
Ms. Verma was silent. Then she smiled. "Everyone writes advanced. You're the first to tell me the truth."
"We can teach Excel," she said. "We can't teach that."
Ace got the job. With a Friday deadline, and a very wet shirt.
Pride says: fake it until they check. Humility says: say it before they check.
What are you pretending to know? Every fall begins with one proud decision.
