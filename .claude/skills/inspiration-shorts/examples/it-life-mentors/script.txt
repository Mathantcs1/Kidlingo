Day one for a fresher in two thousand fifteen. A senior sits next to him and says, "Come, I will show you everything."
The senior shows the code. The senior shows the shortcuts. He even tells him which questions to ask, and which ones to keep for later. The fresher writes everything in a notebook. That was how knowledge moved.
Now it is two thousand twenty-six. Another fresher joins. But this one already knows five tools I have not even opened yet.
In ten minutes, he teaches me a tool that would have taken me a week. And I teach him other things. How to read a meeting. Who to copy on an email. How to say no politely. And what not to say on a client call.
He gives me the tools. I give him the survival guide. Honestly, I think this is a fair trade. We are both senior in different ways.
Tell me, who taught you something new this year? And what did you teach them back?
