In two thousand fifteen, I walked into the Resource Management room and felt calm.
The bench was not a problem. It was a waiting room. I knew a project was coming. Maybe next week, maybe next month, but it was coming. So I learned something new. A tool, a certification, a platform feature. Honestly, some of my best skills came from the bench.
Now it is two thousand twenty-six. The same room. The same chairs.
But every day feels like a countdown now. Everyone is quiet. Everyone is checking their phone. And the thought that nobody says out loud is, "Is this still a waiting room, or is it notice period?"
I do not have the answer to that. But the bench taught me one thing in two thousand fifteen, and I still use it. Use the time. Learn one thing every week. Skills are the only thing that move with you, from one project to the next.
The bench did not change. The confidence did. What did you do with your bench time?
