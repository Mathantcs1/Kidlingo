Six rounds. Two hundred other people applying. And then the email: "We have decided to move forward with other candidates."
In twenty fifteen, my interview was one round. Maybe two. And they asked me about my work. They asked about my projects. I explained my design on a piece of paper. We talked like two engineers. If I knew my work, I had a real chance. At the end, they said, "Can you join in two weeks?"
Now it is twenty twenty-six. Same door. An online test. A coding round. A system design round. A behavioral round. A take-home task. And a culture fit call.
I used to think, "Maybe I got worse." But I did not get worse. The door got narrower. And preparing for six rounds made me better at explaining my own work than I ever was in twenty fifteen.
Tell me, how many rounds was your longest interview?
