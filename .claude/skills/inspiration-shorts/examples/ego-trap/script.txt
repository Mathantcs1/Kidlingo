A fifty million dollar company laughed at a fifty thousand dollar one. Three years later, the joke bought him. Marcus ran Halden Logistics. Four hundred people. Every truck tracked by a two thousand dollar box. Two founders in a garage showed him a phone app that did it for free. He laughed. Cute. Come back with revenue. Year one. Twelve customers became two hundred. Three of his stopped paying for the box. He called it noise. Year two. His own sales team begged him to build an app. We don't chase toys. A customer left. The exit note said: your box needs a truck. Theirs needs a pocket. Year three. Sixty million against nine. An email arrived. We'd like to acquire Halden Logistics. Nine million. He read it twice. The demo he laughed at was now on his own boardroom wall. You don't lose to the giants. You lose to what you wouldn't take seriously.
