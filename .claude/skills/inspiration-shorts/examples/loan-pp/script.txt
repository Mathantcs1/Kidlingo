Four thousand dollars. Twelve months. It grew to seven thousand, one hundred eighty-three. Ace borrowed four thousand dollars to start a candle business. The lender charged five percent every month. Ace told no one. Pride rode in his pocket. Asking for help means you failed. Month three: about four thousand, six hundred thirty. Ace skipped lunch. Sam noticed. You're quiet. Everything okay? Fine, said Ace. Business is booming. Pride smirked. Month six: five thousand, three hundred sixty. The interest that month alone: about two hundred fifty-five dollars. Month nine: about six thousand, two hundred five. Ace stopped answering Sam's calls. Pride kept a spreadsheet of excuses. Month twelve: seven thousand, one hundred eighty-three. Ace opened the lender's letter and sat on the kitchen floor. Sam found him there. She read the number and did not say I told you so. She said, Why didn't you tell me? Ace said, I didn't want you to know I couldn't do it. Sam had eight thousand dollars saved. She lent Ace seven thousand, two hundred, at zero percent. He paid the lender that afternoon. He repaid Sam three hundred dollars a month, for two years. She never charged a cent. Pride calculated the cost of silence: three thousand, one hundred eighty-three dollars. Pride says: hide it. Humility says: ask the person who would have helped for free. What are you hiding because you're too proud to ask? Every fall begins with one proud decision.
