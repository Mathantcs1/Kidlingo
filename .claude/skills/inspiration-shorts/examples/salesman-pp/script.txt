Ace pitched thirty clients in one month. He closed two. Ace sold software to cafés. His pitch was perfect: forty minutes, eleven slides, not one pause. Pride sat on his briefcase. Questions make you look unprepared. Never ask. Tell. Week one: eight pitches, zero deals. Ace blamed the economy. On Thursday, Coach Mira rode along and counted. At the end she showed him her notepad. Words from Ace: all of them. Questions from Ace: zero. The café owner, Priya, finally said, You haven't asked me anything. Pride hissed, Don't. Asking is weakness. Ace took a breath. What's the real problem? Priya blinked. Saturday at ten. The queue goes out the door, and half of them leave. His software had a feature for exactly that. Slide nine. He had skipped it every single time. He closed Priya in nine minutes. Next month, Ace asked three questions in every pitch. Thirty pitches. Eleven deals. Pride asked one question that month: Why does nobody see my genius? Nobody answered. Pride says: telling proves you know. Humility says: asking is how you find out. What question have you been too proud to ask? Every fall begins with one proud decision.
