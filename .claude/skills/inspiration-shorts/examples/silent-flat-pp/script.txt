Three flatmates. One broken geyser. Nobody called.
Day one. The water went cold. Ace said nothing. Pride, perched on his shoulder, smiled. "Whoever calls first looks like the one who cares most. Never be that person."
Sam stood in the doorway in a towel, shivering, and said nothing either. Kabir wrote "geyser" on the whiteboard and went to bed.
Day three. Ace washed his hair in the sink. "Heroic," said Pride.
Day six. Three phones. Three plumbers' numbers saved. Not one call.
Pride kept score. Cold showers: nine. Courage: zero.
Day nine. Kabir finally whispered, "Okay. I've had his number since Tuesday."
Sam looked up. "Since Monday."
They both looked at Ace. "Since day one," said Ace.
Ace pressed call. "Hi. Our geyser is broken. Can you come today?"
The plumber arrived at four. The fix took twenty minutes. The part cost eleven dollars.
Hot water by five. Pride sat in the steam, deeply insulted.
Pride says: whoever calls first loses. Humility says: whoever calls first showers first.
Who is waiting for someone else to make the call? Every fall begins with one proud decision.
