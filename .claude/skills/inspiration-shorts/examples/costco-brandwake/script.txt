The rule stuck. Today's CEO, Ron Vachris, said in March: the hot dog price will not change as long as I'm around.
So where does Costco actually make its money? Not at the food court. In the year that ended this August, Costco collected five point nine billion dollars in membership fees. Its entire operating profit was eleven point seven billion. The fees alone are about half.
