In December two thousand nine, Domino's put its own customers on television, saying its pizza was bad. One said the crust tasted like cardboard. Another said the sauce tasted like ketchup. The company had spent about eighteen months reworking the recipe first, the Associated Press reported. A bolder sauce. A new cheese blend. A garlic and herb crust. Then it ran the ads anyway. Its CEO, Patrick Doyle, told Time in twenty eleven he had a knot in his stomach the day they launched. Domestic same-store sales rose one point four percent in the fourth quarter of two thousand nine. In the first quarter of twenty ten: fourteen point three percent. The stock: closing prices between seven dollars thirty and nine dollars thirty-seven in the launch quarter. Twenty-eight dollars by October twenty eleven. Nearly four hundred eighty by October twenty twenty-one. Do not give the pizza all the credit. Online ordering and delivery tools grew too, an analyst told CNBC.
