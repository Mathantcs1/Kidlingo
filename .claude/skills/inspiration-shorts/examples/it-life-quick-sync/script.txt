Friday. Four forty-five. I am closing my laptop, and a calendar invite appears. The title says, "Quick Sync."
No agenda. No description. My manager, and three names I do not know. And I already know, the way you know a storm is coming, that this is not going to be quick.
So the guessing game starts. Is it a production issue? A new deadline? A reorganization? I open my laptop again and read every email from the last week, just in case I did something wrong.
I join the call. Everyone is smiling. My manager says, "Thanks for joining. We just need to pick a date for the team lunch." Team lunch. My heart was racing for a team lunch.
In two thousand fifteen, a quick sync on Friday meant a production issue. In two thousand twenty-six, it can also mean a reorg. So now we carry a small fear into every invite, even the harmless ones.
Tell me, what was your worst quick sync? And did it turn out to be lunch?
