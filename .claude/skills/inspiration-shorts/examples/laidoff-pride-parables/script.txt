He left for work at nine. He had no work.
Ace got laid off on Monday. On Tuesday, he put on the same shirt, packed the same bag, and walked out the door like nothing happened.
Pride sat on his shoulder, very pleased with itself. "Don't worry anyone. You'll have a new job by Friday."
Friday came. So did the rent.
Ace spent his days at the library, pretending to be busy. Forty-one applications. Two replies. Both said no.
His savings dropped. Forty-eight hundred dollars. Thirty-six hundred. Twenty-four hundred.
Pride loved it. "See? Nobody suspects a thing."
Day twenty-three. A voice behind him said, "That's the same shirt."
It was Sam, his flatmate.
"You leave at nine," Sam said. "You've been at this table since eight-thirty. For three weeks."
Pride whispered, "Lie. Quick."
Ace looked at his screen. Then at Sam. And for once, he ignored the advice. "I got laid off," he said. "I didn't want you to worry."
Sam was quiet for a second. Then he said, "I was worried anyway. Now I can help."
He made one call. Then another. By Friday, Ace had two interviews.
On day thirty-one, Ace got the job. The savings stopped dropping.
Pride sulked in the corner.
Pride says: I'll tell them when it's fixed. Humility says: I'll tell them, so we can fix it.
Who haven't you told? Tell one person today. Every fall begins with one proud decision.
