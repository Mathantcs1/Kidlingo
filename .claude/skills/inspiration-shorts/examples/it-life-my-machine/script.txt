Client demo. Ten o'clock. Everything worked perfectly last night, on my machine.
I tested it five times. Every button worked. Every screen loaded. I even had a backup of the backup. I walked into that room like I was about to launch a rocket.
I clicked the first button. A loader, spinning. Then an error. Five people leaned forward, looking at the screen. I said the sentence every engineer says at least once in their life. "It works on my machine."
The client's environment had a different version of one small thing. Just one. Same code, different world. The manager smiled politely and said, "Let's reschedule." And I smiled back, with my whole body on fire inside.
Since that day, my rule is simple. Never demo in your own world. Always test in the client's world first. At least once.
Everyone has one of these stories. What was yours?
