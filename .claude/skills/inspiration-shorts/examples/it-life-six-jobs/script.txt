In two thousand fifteen, my job title was "Developer." And that sentence meant one thing.
One platform. One set of skills. People came to me for exactly that, and I was the person who knew it. Depth was the whole career plan. If you were good at one thing, you were safe.
In two thousand twenty-six, my job title is still "Developer." The title did not change. The job did.
Front end. Back end. Database. Cloud. Pipelines. Security. And of course, a little AI on the side. One job description, and somehow, seven jobs. And when I read a new job post, I laugh a little, because it asks for ten years of experience in a technology that is three years old.
But here is what I have learned. Depth did not lose its value. It is just not enough alone. The people doing best now have one deep skill and a wide base around it. Depth got me here. Breadth is what keeps me here.
Tell me, how many roles is your job title doing right now?
