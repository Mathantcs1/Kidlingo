In two thousand fifteen, my manager gave me one instruction at appraisal time. "Focus on the problem in your project."
That was easy. One project. One problem. I knew exactly what to do. I fixed the defect, I wrote my notes, and I went home on time. The appraisal talk took fifteen minutes, and everybody was happy.
Now it is two thousand twenty-six. Same meeting room. Same manager. But a different instruction. "Focus on the company."
Sorry. I mean the industry. Sorry, the market. Sorry... the entire universe. And I am sitting there thinking, last year I only had one bug.
To be fair, my manager is getting the same message from above. Everyone is being asked to think bigger, with fewer people and less time. So I try. I fix my bug, and I also try to understand the universe.
Tell me in the comments. What is the biggest thing your appraisal ever asked you to own?
