He replied to a stranger. Four million people watched. Tuesday, nine p.m. Ace saw a post from an account called Grandma Lata. A photo of her first loaf of bread. Her caption said: baked at three fifty for forty minutes. She hadn't written which degrees. Ace noticed. Pride lit up. She's vague in public. Correct her in public. Ace typed: Imagine thinking you can bake. Three fifty Celsius would melt your oven. He hit send. Pride sat back. Perfect. By midnight: ten thousand views. By morning: one million. By lunch: four million. Most of the replies were about him. Not one agreed. Ace's phone buzzed. His boss. Is that you? Our client follows her. Pride whispered, Don't delete it. Deleting is losing. Ace deleted nothing. Views climbed another hundred thousand. He wrote an apology instead. I was right about the number, and wrong about everything else. I'm sorry. Grandma Lata replied in four minutes. Come bake with me. Bring your own flour. Saturday, her kitchen. Ace burned the first loaf. She laughed for a full minute. Pride, watching from the windowsill, discovered he was not invited to the second loaf. Pride says: win the argument. Humility says: win the person. Have you ever been right and still lost? Every fall begins with one proud decision.
