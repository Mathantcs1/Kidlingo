# Titles and upload pack (patterns seen in the reference channels, adapted honestly)

## Title formulas (first 40 characters carry the hook; aim under ~65 characters)
- Person + twist: "The Man Who Turned $400 Into $200 Million"; e.g. "The Gate Was Never Locked"
- Contrast with a series prefix: "Overthinker vs Doer: Same Stone, 100 Days"; e.g. "Pride vs Humility: Same Advice, 365 Days"
- Cliffhanger with ellipsis: "He Laughed at a Tiny Startup. 3 Years Later..."
- First-person / second-person: "I Woke Up on a Gigantic Ship..." ; e.g. "You've Been Lost for 2 Hours. You Still Won't Ask"
- Premise + "|": "If Oceans Had Sound 🔊 | Wait for the Mariana Trench"
- Question/quiz: "Which Room Would You Pick? 👇"
Rules: one relevant emoji at most; a number or concrete noun beats vague words; never promise what the video does not show; keep a consistent series prefix for repeatable styles; hashtags (3 max, #shorts first) go in the description (some channels put them in the title; optional).

## Description template
Line 1 = hook sentence. Line 2 = one-line story or question. Line 3 = call to action (comment A/B, "which would you pick?"). Then 3 hashtags. Then disclosures: "All stories are fictional." / "Narration is AI-generated." / sources for real stories.

## Per-video upload pack
title (+1 alt) | description | 8-12 tags | pinned comment question | cover/thumbnail frame time | publish slot | playlist | "made for kids" = No | altered/synthetic content disclosure answered truthfully.

## Upload steps (YouTube Studio, manual: no upload connector is available to Claude)
Create > Upload videos > choose the 9:16 mp4 (Shorts: vertical, up to 3 minutes) > paste title and description > Audience: not made for kids > More options: altered content disclosure, tags > Visibility: Schedule > Publish. After publishing: pin the comment, reply to the first comments, check retention in Analytics after 24-48h, repeat winners.
Best practice: one Short every 1-2 days, same time slot, mix styles, do not repost the same story twice.
